# Area_cal

A simple C++ program to calculate the **area** and **volume** of various shapes.  

---

## Features

- **Area Calculations** for:
  - Circle
  - Rectangle
  - Square
  - Sector
  - Cube (TSA/LSA)
  - Cuboid (TSA/LSA)
  - Cylinder (TSA/CSA)
  - Sphere

- **Volume Calculations** for:
  - Cube
  - Cuboid
  - Cylinder
  - Cone
  - Sphere
