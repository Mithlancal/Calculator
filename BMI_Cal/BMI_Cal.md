# BMI Calculator

A simple **C++ console program** to calculate Body Mass Index (BMI) based on weight and height, categorize the result, and provide health suggestions.

---

## Features

- Calculates BMI
- Classifies BMI into four categories:
- Underweight
- Normal Weight
- Overweight
- Obese

- Provides remarks and health suggestions based on BMI.
- Displays BMI value with one decimal place for clarity.
- Interactive console interface.

---

## BMI Categories

| BMI Range       | Category        | Remark                                |
|-----------------|----------------|--------------------------------------|
| Below 18.5      | Underweight     | You may need better nutrition.       |
| 18.5 – 24.9     | Normal Weight  | Great! Your weight is healthy.       |
| 25.0 – 29.9     | Overweight     | You may have extra body weight.      |
| 30.0 and above  | Obese          | Your health risk may be higher.      |
